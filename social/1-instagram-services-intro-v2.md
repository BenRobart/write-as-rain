## Notes

- Hashtags are CamelCase on purpose. Screen readers pronounce #BookEditor as two
  words and #bookeditor as one long noise, and it costs nothing to do it right.
- Instagram allows 30 hashtags. Thirty is worse than fourteen: broad tags like
  #writing put you against millions of posts you cannot compete in, and the
  scattergun reads as spam to actual people. The set above leans on tags where a
  small account can still surface.
- #FreeSampleEdit has almost no volume. It's in there because it's the thing
  worth being found for, and it costs one slot.
- Prices are checked against index.html and pricing.html as of 5 August 2026.
  If you change a price on the site, this caption goes stale.
- The book ghostwriting sentence is the £3,500 tier (up to 30,000 words). The
  £450 chapter plan and the £7,000 tier are deliberately left for the website;
  three numbers for one service in a caption reads like a price list.
- The graphic's Manuscript editing card had to buy the room for ghostwriting.
  Blurbs, formatting, and agent submissions came off the card, and "formatting
  from £75" came off its price line, because the card is capped at three lines
  of body text – a fourth pushes the closing promise through the frame and into
  the burgundy bar. All four are still in the caption and on the site. If you
  change that card, re-measure rather than eyeballing it.
- The domain in the footer bar stays bare. It's on an image, so it can only be
  typed, never tapped: a tracked /ig_ path there would be longer to type and
  would attribute nothing, since nobody arriving that way came through the
  redirect. The tracked link for this post is in the Link section above.
- The caption is British English throughout, uses the Oxford comma, and uses
  spaced en dashes rather than em dashes, per the client AI guidelines.
- No emoji, for the same reason the bio has none.
