# Instagram bio

Instagram gives you three separate fields. The limits are hard, and they count
line breaks and spaces.

| Field | Limit | Searchable? |
|---|---|---|
| Name | 30 characters | Yes – this is the field Instagram searches |
| Bio | 150 characters | No |
| Link | separate field | n/a |

The Name field is the one that gets matched against searches, so it should carry
the words someone would actually type. "Write as Rain" alone wastes it.

---

## Name field

Pick one. Counts are exact.

- `Write as Rain | Editor` (22)
- `Write as Rain · Book Editor` (27)
- `Quinn | Editor & Ghostwriter` (28)

Recommended: **`Write as Rain · Book Editor`**. It keeps the brand first and puts
"book editor" in the searchable field, which is what people type.

---

## Bio options

### A – the site's own line (111 characters)

```
Every word deserves a second pair of eyes.
Proofreading, copyediting, and ghostwriting.
Free sample edit below.
```

The line the whole site is built on, so anyone arriving from a post or the
website gets the same sentence twice. Safest option.

### B – what you get, plainly (149 characters)

```
Manuscripts, applications, and business writing.
One editor, not an agency. Flat prices, tracked changes.
Free sample edit on your first 1,000 words.
```

Answers "what do I get and what does it cost me" without a single adjective.
Sits one character under the limit, so it cannot take an edit without a cut.

### C – shortest, most personal (115 characters)

```
I'm Quinn. I edit and ghostwrite, on my own, in the UK.
Your voice, made stronger.
Free sample edit before you pay.
```

Uses the "your voice, made stronger" line from the About section. Best if the
account is going to sound like a person rather than a business.

---

Recommended: **B**, with **A** as the fallback. B is the only one that mentions
price behaviour, and "flat prices" plus "free sample edit" is the thing that
separates you from every editing account that leads with a stock photo of a
laptop.

## Link field

`https://writeasrain.github.io`

Don't spend bio characters on the URL. The link field is separate and doesn't
count against your 150.

## Notes

- No emoji in any of these. The site is cream paper, burgundy ink, and Playfair
  Display; emoji would be the only thing on the whole account that isn't.
- All three are British English and use the Oxford comma, matching the site.
- Line breaks work in Instagram bios but are stripped if you paste into the
  mobile app's edit field from some keyboards. Paste on desktop, or type the
  breaks in manually.
- Whichever you choose, keep "free sample edit" in it. It's the strongest thing
  you offer and it's the only claim on the site that costs a reader nothing to
  test.
