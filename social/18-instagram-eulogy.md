## Notes

- **The grief tags are left out on purpose.** #Grief, #GriefSupport,
  #GriefJourney, #InLovingMemory and the rest are where bereaved people talk to
  each other, not where they shop. Turning up in those feeds with a service to
  sell is the kind of thing people screenshot and repost angrily, and rightly.
  The set above sticks to tags used by someone actively looking for help with a
  task: eulogy, funeral planning, order of service. Fewer tags, no risk.
- The caption opens with condolence and never once uses a sales line. No "get in
  touch today", no urgency, no exclamation mark. Anything that reads as
  marketing here costs more than it earns.
- The offer to talk it through for free and take no money is real and should
  stay. Some of these calls should end with the person writing it themselves,
  and that has to be an available outcome rather than a rhetorical flourish.
- The candle icon is the quietest drawing in the sprite. Don't swap it for
  anything with more personality.
- Post this one on its own, not on the same day as the wedding speeches post.
