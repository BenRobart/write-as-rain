## Notes

- Hashtags are CamelCase on purpose. Screen readers pronounce #BookEditor as two
  words and #bookeditor as one long noise, and it costs nothing to do it right.
- Instagram allows 30 hashtags. Thirty is worse than fourteen: broad tags like
  #writing put you against millions of posts you cannot compete in, and the
  scattergun reads as spam to actual people. The set above leans on tags where a
  small account can still surface.
- #FreeSampleEdit has almost no volume. It's in there because it's the thing
  worth being found for, and it costs one slot.
- Prices are checked against index.html and pricing.html as of 3 August 2026.
  If you change a price on the site, this caption goes stale.
- The caption is British English throughout, uses the Oxford comma, and uses
  spaced en dashes rather than em dashes, per the client AI guidelines.
- No emoji, for the same reason the bio has none.
